# Source-backed issuer revenue reconciliation

## Demand and evidence

The receiving business operation requires an autonomous product and procurement
experiment with no new cash purchase, useful public fulfillment and an
independently measured USD20/day earning objective. Business operations and
technology must derive from real research and remain integrated into their
ordinary source ecosystem. Technical polish, temporary deployment assemblies
and unpublished proposal branches do not establish that outcome. The original
authority and exact unabridged demand remain in the Group's governing Work
and private execution context; this public supplier source carries only the
resulting product requirements.

This supplier is one Engineering obligation in that larger operating demand;
it cannot establish the earning goal or accept its own commercial outcome.
The Group owns product, price, budget, customer outcome and selection. Coesite
owns repeatable procurement/fulfillment rules; Public owns seller configuration
and the actual customer offer. Science owns the primary findings in
`science-services-section/services/social-science-services-section/services/economics-and-business-science-services-section/services/economics-science-services-section/data/records/2026-10-24-autonomous-agent-service-demand.json`.

The primary paid-buyer study
[Answerpool revenue stale tag](https://whatagentsbuy.com/p/answerpool-revenue-stale-tag)
identifies a specific failed job: a paid revenue lookup returns an obsolete
SEC concept as current. Direct SEC observations in the Science record show
Apple's old Revenues concept ending in 2018 while its successor reaches 2026;
Microsoft has annual and quarterly facts that cannot be compared solely by
filing fiscal-year labels. This supports a correctness problem and existence of
some paid demand. It does not establish our price, conversion, retention,
independent buyer identity, or USD20/day.

## Requirements

- R1: Reconcile explicitly identified SEC issuers, requested quarter or annual
  duration, and an as-of date through a pure reusable kernel. The first public
  product serves a fixed two-issuer cohort prepared by its operating owner;
  customer input selects quarter or annual, never arbitrary source egress.
  Produce source-backed period/revision reconciliation without inference/trading.
- R2: Acquire only official SEC companyfacts using an independently configured
  self-identifying User-Agent. Refuse invalid requests before egress. Bound
  requests, response bytes, timeout, rate and retries under the actual shared
  SEC requester policy. One existing durable coordinator acquires the fixed
  cohort on its existing cadence, then writes one compact prepared object
  atomically; customer calls read that object and issue no SEC requests.
  Module-local spacing alone is not a global rate proof.
- R3: Inspect current and legacy revenue concepts together; retain concept,
  unit, start/end, duration, form, filed date and accession for every selected
  observation. Never label a legacy fact current just because it is present.
- R4: Distinguish quarter, year-to-date and annual facts from start/end;
  fy/fp describe filing context and cannot substitute for a fact's period.
  No implicit Q4 subtraction, sums of competing concepts or invented fact.
- R5: Preserve same-period report/revision witnesses. Distinguish changed
  reported value from equal-value later reporting without asserting an
  accounting restatement. Expose unresolved same-date/concept conflicts.
- R6: Bind claims to received source bytes, observation time and dated
  provenance through the existing formIssuerEvidencePacket identity supplier;
  preserve its public API and identity behavior.
- R7: Report missing, stale, incompatible and ambiguous periods explicitly.
  No annual-versus-quarter ranking; no precision beyond SEC filing dates.
  Source unavailability is a retryable refusal, not invented completeness.
- R8: Integrate the supplier, consumer binding and public offer in the actual
  ecosystem. A source proposal, native assay and public paid acceptance are
  different receiving conditions. No temporary deployment composition.

## Verification and boundaries

The native packet identity regression stays intact. Planned original-consumer
assays cover AAPL obsolete/current concepts, MSFT repeated historical facts in
a newer filing, quarter versus nine-month and annual, different concept values,
same-period new filings, date cutoffs, stale/missing observations, invalid and
oversized issuer lists, repeated issuer, SEC denial/rate limiting, response
bounds and self-identification. Live source readback is a separate no-payment
receiving assay. Group receiving must independently observe actual customer
acceptance and cost headroom before claiming income or scalable capacity.
